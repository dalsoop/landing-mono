cuAlign research prototype · Version 2026-09-28

In short: cuAlign is a hackathon research prototype. Use it for research and demonstration only, never for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Its plans are drafts, a dentist stays responsible for every clinical decision, and the software comes without warranty. Do not upload identifiable patient data.

1. Agreement

By installing, running or otherwise using the cuAlign software, including its web interface, command-line tools and generated files (together, “the Software”), you agree to this Agreement. If you do not agree, do not use the Software.

2. Research and demonstration use only

The Software is a research prototype built for the NVIDIA Hackathon 2026. You may use it only to research, evaluate and demonstrate its behaviour. It is not a medical device, has not been cleared or approved by any regulator, and has not been clinically validated.

3. No clinical use

  • Do not use the Software, or anything it produces, to diagnose, plan or carry out treatment of any person.
  • Staging plans, rule-check results, reviewer notes and exported files are drafts for review. They may be wrong or incomplete.
  • Exported STL files and printable models are for reviewing plans. They are not validated for manufacturing and are not wearable aligners.
  • Any clinical decision remains the sole responsibility of a licensed dentist.

4. Known limits

The Software currently handles the upper arch only and models tooth translation, incisor rotation correction and vertical correction. It does not model rotation of canines, premolars or molars, torque, the lower arch, occlusion or tissue response. Stage counts convert to time with a fixed wear interval and do not predict treatment duration. The current limits are listed in the project documentation.

5. Patient data

  • Do not upload data that identifies a person, including names, national ID numbers, phone numbers or e-mail addresses.
  • Use pseudonyms for patient records. The Software stores patient records and scans on the machine that runs its server.
  • The server has no user authentication or per-user isolation. Run it in a local, trusted environment.
  • When the agent conversation is enabled, your messages and case context are sent to the configured NVIDIA model endpoint under your own API key.
